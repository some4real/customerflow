FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can we get started?

You can be up and running in under 5 minutes. Simply create an account, set up your workflow stages, and add your customers. We provide templates to help you get started immediately.

Can we customize the notification messages?

Absolutely! You have full control over the message templates for each stage. Add your company branding, customize tone, and include dynamic fields like customer name, order ID, and estimated delivery time.

What payment options do you support?

We support all major payment methods including credit cards, debit cards, bank transfers, and wire transfers. All billing is handled securely through our payment processor.

Can we migrate data from our existing system?

Yes! Our team provides free migration support. We can import your existing orders, customers, and workflows from any system. Most migrations are completed within 1-2 days.

What's your customer support like?

We offer 24/7 email and chat support for all customers. Premium plans include dedicated account managers and phone support. Average response time is under 2 hours.

Is there a contract or can we cancel anytime?

No long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription anytime. If you're on a monthly plan, you can downgrade or upgrade at any time with changes taking effect on your next billing date.
